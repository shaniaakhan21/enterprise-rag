CONFIDENTIAL LEGAL MEMORANDUM
Matter: Wrongful Termination Claim - Johnson v. TechCorp Inc.
Date: April 2, 2024
Attorney: James R. Henderson, Employment Law
Summary of Facts: Client was terminated on February 28, 2024 after 7 years
of service without documented cause. Client received no severance package.
Client performance reviews for preceding 3 years were all rated Exceeds Expectations.
Recommended Action: File EEOC complaint within 180 days of termination.
Potential damages: Lost wages $127,000 annually plus benefits.
Settlement range: $200,000 to $450,000 based on comparable cases.
